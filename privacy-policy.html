Privacy Policy — FI Countdown

Last updated: October 2026

FI Countdown ("the extension") is a new-tab page that estimates how many years remain until you reach financial independence, using the 4% rule.

Data we collect

None. The extension has no account system, no analytics, no tracking, and makes no network requests. It does not transmit anything anywhere.

Data stored on your device

The numbers you enter (invested assets, annual spending, expected return, monthly savings) are saved only in your own browser via the chrome.storage API so the new-tab page remembers your settings. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the extension removes it.

Permissions

The extension requests only the storage permission (to remember your settings locally) and the ability to replace the new-tab page. It cannot read or change the content of websites you visit.
